## Interpretation decisions (V1–V8), open to red-team before build

- **V1 — surface migrates by file ownership.** `index_assembly` owns
  `analyzer/indexer.py`, so every surface subject defined there moves to it:
  `build_index`, `serialize`, `load_index`, `IndexLoadError`, the `record`
  type, plus the D1-B `import_origins` + imports-table entries. symbol_table
  keeps `extract_symbols`, `module_path`, `ParseFailure`. Verified by index:
  those seven subjects' defining files are exactly as split.
- **V2 — done_when migrates with its subject.** "Indexing the same commit
  twice produces byte identical output" and "The emitted index maps every
  symbol path to at least one record…" are claims about the *index*, so they
  move to index_assembly. "Reformatting whitespace does not change the body
  hash" and "Ranges are recomputed per commit…" are extraction claims and
  stay with symbol_table. Consequence to state plainly: **3d-1's four
  ATTESTs split across the two nodes**; the two index-level ones are
  re-attested under index_assembly's dispatch, the two extraction-level ones
  stand under symbol_table.
- **V3 — references are the closure, not just the new edge.** The ruling
  names `references: [reference_map]`, which is the edge the restructure
  creates. But `indexer.py` also imports `gitio` and `symbols`/`modpath`, so
  declaring only reference_map would fire NEW W05s on those and contradict
  "W05 clears". index_assembly therefore declares
  `[gitio, symbol_table, reference_map]`. **Both readings will be compiled
  and the plan output shown**, so the choice is evidenced rather than
  asserted.
- **V4 — the imports entry is a `types` line on index_assembly**, replacing
  D1-B's prose line, and states the sort rule inline as ruled ("files,
  entries, and origin lists emit sorted"). `import_origins`' contract line
  states that it matches on `module`.
- **V5 — the required fixture becomes a done_when bullet**, since done_when
  is what a tester builds fixtures from: bare `import pkg.impl`, imported
  and never called, yields a module-kind entry whose `origin` and `module`
  are both `pkg.impl`. This is 3d-1's own proof promoted to contract.
- **V6 — F28 has two parts.** (a) *env-bound fence*: the hook resolves
  `.harness/fence.json` from `LEX_FENCE` (absolute path) before falling back
  to the cwd walk, so a subagent whose cwd is the parent repo is still
  bound; `dispatch prepare` emits the variable in the manifest and the
  compiled prompt does not carry it (it is environment for the spawner, not
  the agent). (b) *spawn canary deny*: `dispatch canary` performs a probe
  the fence must refuse and exits nonzero if it is allowed, so a dispatch
  cannot begin against an inert wall. prepare runs it and records the result.
- **V7 — F29 mechanics.** The fence gains `reads` (list of allowed prefixes)
  and `read_policy` (`hard` for tester, `disclose` for executor). The hook
  gains Read/Grep/Glob handling: on `hard`, deny outside `reads`; on
  `disclose`, allow and append a read event to
  `.harness/read_events.jsonl`. Bash reads stay soft under both, with
  disclosure mandatory by protocol text. `dispatch harvest` folds
  `read_events.jsonl` into the dispatch record as `reads_out_of_packet`, and
  counts them as self-served misses in the record's `miss_log`.
- **V8 — protocol text follows the mechanism.** Both roles' Protocol
  sections gain the read rule in one line each, since an unstated rule is
  what produced two opposite readings of the same five words (F29).
