### Proposal C — F25: the seeded-growth policy

The packet currently contains two surface lines in direct tension: `Row` is
`[seeded]` with an exhaustive field list that excludes `callers_unknowable`,
while an `[authored]` line requires that very field. Both probes flagged the
contradiction; neither could resolve it. Proposed rule:

> An `[authored]` line naming an attribute of a `[seeded]` type authorises
> the executor to add exactly that attribute. The `[seeded]` line reseeds
> byte-for-byte after the work lands (implementation-caused reseed, flag (a)
> precedent).
