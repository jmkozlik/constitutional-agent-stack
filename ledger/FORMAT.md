# Ledger entry format

The ledger (`$VAULT_ROOT/ledger/`) is the append-only governance record.
Every run, motion, panel, ratification, promotion, and amendment leaves an
entry. Append-only is not a convention — it is mechanically enforced for
every agent, at every rank, by `hooks/ledger-guard.js` (edits to existing
entries are blocked at the tool layer; only new files can be added).

## Filename

```
NNNN-<slug>.md
```

- `NNNN` — next integer, zero-padded to 4. Numbering is global and
  monotonic; gaps are never reused.
- `<slug>` — what the entry is, kebab-case. Common shapes:
  - `NNNN-<run-id>-start.md` / `NNNN-<run-id>-complete.md` — run lifecycle
  - `NNNN-<run-id>-panel.md` — council plan review
  - `NNNN-motion-<topic>.md` — a filed motion
  - `NNNN-motion-NNNN-adopted.md` / `-resolved.md` — motion outcomes
  - `NNNN-amendment-NNNN-promoted.md` — a law amendment going live

`_index.md` is not an entry; it is mechanically regenerated by
`vaultctl index` and exempt from the guard.

## Entry structure

YAML frontmatter, then a dated body. Bodies are terse — one line where one
line suffices; ` · ` separates fields within a line.

```markdown
---
type: log | motion | decision
labels: [<controlled-vocabulary labels only>]
run-id: <run-id, when the entry belongs to a run>
status: final | filed | ratified | vetoed
---
YYYY-MM-DD · <WHAT HAPPENED> · <key facts> · <artifact citations>
```

Rules the format encodes:

- **Every claim cites artifacts.** Run-ids, contract task-ids, archive
  paths, digest paths, diff references. An uncited ledger line is noise
  (Core Law, Article I).
- **Labels come from the controlled vocabulary** in
  `law/retrieval-guide.md` — nothing else is legal.
- **Run entries are mandatory** at start (lane + rationale + contract list)
  and completion (outcome + verify summary) — see `law/run-protocol.md`.
- **Panel entries record pre-convene and post-convene votes separately**,
  with confidence, so vote changes are auditable against the evidence that
  changed them.
- **Motions record co-sign counts** and point at the anonymized digest they
  came from, never at an individual executor.

See `examples/` for three synthetic entries (a run start, a motion, and a
panel verdict) written against a fictional toy run. They demonstrate the
format only; real ledger entries stay in the private vault.
